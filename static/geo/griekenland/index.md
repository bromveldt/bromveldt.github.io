#### Paxos

You can also visit Mongonissi village which is in the harbour of the island’s southern tip. It is just four kilometres south of Gaios and is linked to Paxos by a small bridge. This is where you can stop off and soak in the views and beautiful sunsets it has to offer.
While many places you go for a break it is fast-paced Paxos offers a slower way of life where you can watch the world go by.

Even better there are 12 beaches you can choose to relax on including, Mesovrika Beach, Voutoumi Beach, Monodendri Beach, Pounda Beach, Logos Beach and Levrehio Beach to name a few.

All of them have crystal blue water and stunning views to take in.

from Corfu, you have to catch a ferry which can take one to two hours to Paxos.  

There are several hotels and apartments available on the island with prices varying at the time of booking.
