#### Culpable for Holocaust

In time it engaged such Jewish theologians as Emil Fackenheim, Eliezer Berkovits and Arthur Green and writers like Elie Wiesel, who had already exposed his own doubts in his Holocaust memoir, “Night.”

Richard Lowell Rubenstein

Her father, Ms. Rubenstein said, was taken aback when he heard Dean Grüber say, “It must be that it was God’s will that Hitler did what he did.”

Dr. Rubenstein, she said, feared that if even someone like Dean Grüber, a man so sympathetic to Jewish concerns, could hold such traditional conceptions of God, others might harbor them as well.

“My father,” she said, echoing his language, “felt that was obscene.”
