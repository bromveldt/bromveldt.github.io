#### Segovia

The Romanesque Church of Veracruz near Segovia, south of the town of Zamarramala (Spain) was built by the Order of the Templar (Templar Knights). Presumably, they copied the plan from the Saint Sepulchre of Jerusalem. The circular building (interior) was consecrated on April 13, 1208 and was known as ‘Santo Sepulchro’ until 1226 when a piece of the ‘True Cross’ was given to the church by Pope Honorius III.

#### Palma

Sometimes it’s nice to just go to a random small town with a bar on the main square, because then you see the locals. If I want a cocktail, I’ll head to Palma: Clandestino and Door 13 are nice, but the place I like most there is Can Bordoy. It’s the best hotel in the city: small and boutique, quite sexy, dimly lit with tables far apart. It also has a great garden, with a pool.

#### Minorca

Deià is still my favourite, but off-season, because in summer it gets really packed. There are several lovely towns on the north coast you could visit: Valldemossa or Sóller, or Port de Pollença on the north-east tip. But the must-see for me is Sa Calobra, a serpentine hiking and cycling route that eventually opens onto the sea. The view is amazing – very alien. You think, “Did I land on Mars?”
