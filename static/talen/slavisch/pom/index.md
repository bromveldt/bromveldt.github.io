Pomor (*поморьска говоря*) — the traditional speech of the Pomors of the Russian North (Arkhangelsk region, White Sea coast).

- [Поморьска говоря](pomor.md) — notes on the Pomor language with a Mezen–Kimzha glossary
- [ПомОрьска говОря](govorya/index.md) — И.И. Мосеев, Краткий словарь поморского языка*(Arkhangelsk, 2005): essays, grammar rules, a ~2500-word dictionary and traditional Pomor fairy tales
