## mtDNA matches on my control region

I have 4074 matches on HVR1 and just 22 on HVR2 + HVR1 and 3 (2 full and 1 with 3 different mutations). It would mean that 20 other people (the one imperfect match is not listed by FamilyTreeDNA among my 22 control region matches) have the most recent common ancestor with me around 5450 BCE. Hardly a genealogical breakthrough.

Wikipedia [writes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neolithic): places the beginning of the Neolitic around 650

> Most of Western Europe followed over the next two thousand years, but in some parts of Northwest Europe the neolithic era began much later and lasted just under 3,000 years from c. 4500 BC–1700 BC.
> Through a combination of cultural diffusion and migration of peoples, the Neolithic traditions spread west and northwards to reach northwestern Europe by around 4500 BC.
> With some exceptions, population levels rose rapidly at the beginning of the Neolithic until they reached the carrying capacity.[58] This was followed by a population crash of "enormous magnitude" after 5000 BC, with levels remaining low during the next 1,500 years.[58]

The article on [Neolithic decline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neolithic_decline):

> The population increase between 5950 and 5550 BP (4000 to 3600 BC) that preceded the decline was catalysed by the introduction of agriculture, along with the spread of technologies such as pottery, the wheel, and animal husbandry. After the Neolithic decline, there were massive human migrations from the Pontic–Caspian steppe into eastern and central Europe, beginning approximately 4800 BP (2850 BC).

Hence all those matches are from the times before the rapid Neolithic expansion.
