The leaders of the Zionist Yishuv were highly selective over which Jews to allow to flee persecution or annihilation. There was no appetite for massive immigration of Jewish refugees and in some instances Zionist Yishuv leaders sought to return Jewish refugees in Palestine to their certain death in Europe during World war 2. I highly recommend Tom Segev's book "The Seventh Million". You may be shocked like me when reading it.
