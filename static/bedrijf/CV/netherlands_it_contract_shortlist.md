Channels for finding Dutch IT contract projects (Java / Spring Boot / Kubernetes, AI-assisted dev) as a Utrecht-based contractor working through Osterholm B.V. — the NL counterpart to the German and Irish shortlists.

**Positioning:** use the EN/EU CV; senior ICT rates on the open marketplaces run €95–110/h (freelance.nl 2024 figures), enterprise brokered rates higher. Language splits the market: the corporate segment (banks, telecom, tech) works in English; the public sector almost always requires working Dutch. With intermediate Dutch, lead with corporates and English-language postings first, and treat the overheid route as the channel that opens up as the Dutch improves.

## A. Marketplaces & aggregators (self-listing, you apply directly)

- **Striive (HeadFirst Group)** — the largest single supply of NL opdrachten; direct route into large corporates and government inhuur. Between Staffing was folded into HeadFirst, so one profile here covers what used to be several channels. Best single starting point.
- **Freelance.nl** — largest generic NL marketplace; free account, no commission on your rate; has a dedicated Java-developer stream.
- **IT-Contracts.nl** — IT-only board (freelance / interim / detachering) with large-client and government postings; searchable by Java.
- **Funle** — aggregator pulling ~40,000 opdrachten/year from 60+ platforms and direct clients; good as a one-stop search on top of the individual boards.
- **Planet Interim** — aggregator with daily auto-matching from 2,500+ clients and 300+ intermediaries.
- **LinkedIn** — ~280 live "freelance Java" postings in NL at time of writing; recruiters here overlap heavily with the brokers below.

## B. Brokers / detacheerders (they hold the client and the contract)

- **HeadFirst** — broker/MSP side of the same group as Striive; sits between many enterprises and their flex workforce.
- **Harvey Nash** — international IT recruitment; places contractors at KPN and similar NL enterprises.
- **StarApple** — NL IT-specialist bureau; project-based placement at scale-ups, corporates and government.
- **iSense** — Java-focused NL bureau; worth being in their database for inbound Java requests.
- **Yacht (Randstad)** and **LINKIT** — additional interim/IT intermediaries worth a profile; Yacht is the NL counterpart of GULP inside the same Randstad group.
- **Computer Futures / SThree** — already on the German shortlist; also active in NL, so one relationship can cover both markets.

## C. Government route (overheid inhuur)

- **Flextender** — independent inhuurdesk/DAS platform; register once, receive matching zzp-opdrachten by mail; covers many gemeenten and public bodies.
- **TenderNed** — the official procurement portal; every dynamic purchasing system (DAS) for personnel inhuur is announced here, so it tells you which inhuurdesk a given organisation uses.
- **Freep.nl / Opdrachten.nl / ZiPconomy's marktplaatsen list** — overviews and aggregation of the public-sector marketplaces (Poolz, Negometrix/Mercell, Nétive, CTM and the rest).
- Practical constraint: postings and interviews here are in Dutch. Realistic once Dutch moves from intermediate toward working-professional.

## Wet DBA — the thing that changed since 2025

Enforcement against schijnzelfstandigheid resumed 1 January 2025; since 1 January 2026 the Belastingdienst can add penalty fines for intent or gross negligence, with correction assessments retroactive to 2025. The January 2026 coalition agreement announced a Zelfstandigenwet to replace part of the planned Vbar rules — the framework is still moving. Market effect: clients are cautious about direct zzp engagement, which pushes work toward brokers and detachering constructions (one more reason section B matters). Working through a BV does not by itself settle the classification question — the test is the actual working relationship (embedding, direction, substitutability), not the legal form. Multiple concurrent clients, own R&D, own equipment and result-based statements of work all help.

## How to work the list

1. Profile on **Striive** and **Freelance.nl** first; add **Funle** as the umbrella search.
2. Send the EN CV to 3–4 brokers (HeadFirst, Harvey Nash, StarApple, iSense) for inbound flow.
3. Register with **Flextender** now even if the overheid route only becomes realistic later — the mail flow doubles as market intelligence on rates and demand.
4. For each engagement, have the wet-DBA answers ready before procurement asks: an NL-facing client note (the counterpart of the German *Kundenhinweis* and the Irish client note) covering classification, VAT and the BV structure.
