Channels for finding German IT contract projects (Java / Spring Boot / Kubernetes, AI-assisted dev) as a Netherlands-based freelancer working remotely through Osterholm B.V.

**Positioning across all of them:** use the German CV, set availability to *remote / hybrid*, and lead on the current stack. For agencies, the contract typically sits between Osterholm B.V. and the agency, so they absorb the reverse-charge / AÜG paperwork — hand them the *Kundenhinweis* one-pager if questions come up. Maintain a profile on the self-listing marketplaces (outbound) **and** with a few agencies (inbound).

## A. Self-listing marketplaces (you control the profile, apply directly)

- **freelancermap.de** — largest DACH marketplace, 2,000+ new projects per week; create a profile, filter for remote, apply directly or get found by Vermittler. Best single starting point.
- **Malt (malt.de)** — pan-European platform (DE / FR / ES) built for cross-border engagements; handles contracting, invoicing and payment across borders, which fits a Dutch BV billing German clients particularly well. Strong remote culture.
- **freelance.de** — large German portal, IT/SAP-heavy; similar self-listing model to freelancermap.
- **it-projekte.de** — board/aggregator connecting freelancers, Vermittler and clients.

## B. Agencies / Projektvermittler (recruiter contacts you; they hold the client and the contract)

- **GULP (Randstad Professional)** — one of the largest; GULP project portal plus GULP Direkt; enterprise clients; transparent placement model.
- **SOLCOM** — IT/engineering broker across DE/AT/CH; recruiter-driven; remote projects available.
- **Hays / tribeworks** — very large staffing group; *tribeworks* is Hays' IT-freelancer portal aimed at SMEs; enterprise reach through Hays itself.
- **Etengo** — specialist high-end IT-freelance Projektdienstleister; enterprise clients; used to compliant cross-border contracting.
- **Westhouse** — international project provider for large/enterprise clients across the EU; comfortable with international contractors.
- **Computer Futures / SThree** — international IT recruitment group; routinely places EU-based and remote contractors.
- **Allgeier, Goetzfried, Questax** — additional IT Vermittler worth a profile.

## How to work the list

1. Build profiles on **freelancermap** and **Malt** first — they're your self-driven funnel and the most remote/cross-border friendly.
2. Send the German CV to 3–5 agencies (GULP, SOLCOM, Hays/tribeworks, Etengo, Westhouse) — these are inbound; once you're in their database, projects come to you.
3. When an agency or client raises VAT or false-self-employment concerns, send the *Kundenhinweis* one-pager; it answers the standard procurement questions in one page.
4. Confirm *remote / cross-border* is acceptable per project — some Vermittler still default to on-site German-resident contractors.
